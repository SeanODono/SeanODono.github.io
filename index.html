About

I love shooting landscapes, especially along the rugged coast and green hills of West Cork in Ireland and the cliffs and sunsets of Isla Vista. Some of my favorite shots come from road trips, where every stop is a chance to find a new view. Closer to home, I photograph my friends' band shows, capturing the energy of live music from the crowd and the stage.
